Revitalizing the Kanke Reservoir Water Supply System

A collaborative initiative to restore clean water access for 15,000 residents in the Kanke block, utilizing IoT-based monitoring and community-driven maintenance protocols.

warning

The Original Challenge

The Kanke reservoir faced critical contamination levels and severe structural leaks in the primary distribution pipelines. Citizen reports highlighted a 40% reduction in water pressure and visible discoloration over a three-month period.

  • 15,000+ citizens affected daily.
  • 40% pressure drop in Sector B pipelines.
  • High coliform bacteria count detected in preliminary tests.
build

The Solution

IoT Sensor Deployment

Installation of 24 low-cost IoT turbidity and flow sensors at critical junctions, providing real-time data to the central dashboard.

Pipeline Rehabilitation

Targeted replacement of 2.4km of decaying cast-iron pipes with durable HDPE, guided by predictive flow modeling.

Bio-filtration Units

Implementation of native-plant bio-filtration systems at the reservoir inlet to naturally reduce initial sediment load.

Community Training

Workshops for 50 local volunteers on maintaining the bio-filters and reporting structural issues via the Sahyog app.
